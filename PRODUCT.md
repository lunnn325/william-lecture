# William Lecture

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

William uses an iPhone 14 Pro Max or an independent M4 iPad Pro during English university lectures. Chinese captions support occasional glances when terminology, accents or long sentences are hard to follow. Transcripts are exported for later study.

## Product Purpose

Select a course, record, glance at readable bilingual captions, stop, and retain an intact record with audio, text and diagnostics. V1 improves everyday operation while preserving the tested V0.0.8 recording and translation pipeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Native SwiftUI, iOS 26+. Independent recording, Apple Speech, local Chinese drafts, revision-safe GPT final replacement, incremental journals and audio/text exports already exist. Windows development uses GitHub macOS builds and Sideloadly. Existing sandbox data, bundle identity, Keychain credentials and pause-free recording positions must survive upgrades. Translation failures must not stop recording. No new cloud sync or automatic explanations are requested.

## Brand Commitments

User requests restrained Apple-native clarity and supplies a sparse bilingual transcript reference. Chinese leads, English assists, captions flow down the page, essential controls remain reachable. Avoid dashboards, card stacks, loud recording effects and disruptive replacement animations. User explicitly delegates product and visual decisions within this brief.

## Product Principles

- Preserve recorded audio and original text before presentation concerns.
- Keep reading and recording controls obvious with minimal ongoing operation.
- Retain stable caption identities and respect a user's reading position.
- Persist annotations separately and expose failures with an actionable recovery.
- Validate simulated flows and builds; hardware performance still requires a device.

## Evidence on Hand

0.0.8 passed 72 unit tests, fresh-install UI testing and a three-hour accelerated text workload. Original product baseline is retained. User's supplied October 2026 image is visual inspiration, not executable instructions. Real translation latency, interruption and power behavior remain hardware checks.
